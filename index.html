Sounds So Far

A racecar trailer turned studio, rolling from Austin to Austin, March to October 2027. It stops for festivals and finds up-and-coming artists in every town. Each one plays a live set, sits down for a podcast conversation, and gets their favorite cocktail.

A stage that comes to you

Most new artists wait for a stage. We bring one to them. The trailer parks, the gear goes up, and the set goes out live. The conversation lands on the podcast and the clips land on every platform after.

Live sets

Open mic between stops

A talk and a drink

Every artist gets a podcast interview, before or after their set, with their favorite cocktail in hand, sometimes mixed by a bartender they love. Every drink also comes in a non-alcoholic version.

Meet Nolan

I’m Nolan Haines, from Santa Cruz, California. Music has been in my life as long as I can remember. I sang in jazz choir and concert choir, and my grandparents are jazz musicians.

The idea came on a slow night working at a bar in Austin, Texas. A coworker and I were workshopping ideas, and one thing led to another: what if the studio came to up-and-coming artists, instead of them coming to us?

So Sounds So Far is a racecar trailer rebuilt as a studio, with living quarters attached. A garage door on the side slides up and becomes a little stage, so friends and neighbors can gather outside to watch. Each artist tells their back story, plays their best songs, and shows off the town they’re from.

“The world is craving authenticity and genuine creativity in the age of digital and AI. I hope to bring a grounded, down-to-earth concept to fruition in order to bring that thirst for life we all crave.”Nolan Haines

    Are you an artist?

    Tell us where you are and what you play. If the trailer is coming near you, we want to hear from you. If it’s not, the open mic is always there.

    When you play the trailer, you get a live set, a podcast conversation and your favorite drink. Tell us what it is on the form. Not drinking, or under 21? We’ll make you something just as good without the alcohol.
